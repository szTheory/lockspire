| Category | Required outcome | Scope control |
|---|---|---|
| Qualification | Maintainer can reproduce/classify retained findings at explicit identities and select a finite supported-case ledger | Historical suite_failure does not establish a present bug |
| Authorization responses | Clients receive the correct safe response for selected redirect/error/state/response-mode cases | Preserve exact-match and reject unsafe redirects; expected behavior comes from applicable standards |

| Package and handoff | Selected cases have automated package-level evidence and required candidate gates; maintainers receive a coherent clean delivery handoff | Supplemental suite remains non-certifying; integration/publication distinct |
