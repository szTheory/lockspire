**Technical disposition and review resolution are separate.** `Current tier` and `Disposition` describe machine evidence only. `Resolution` describes whether a claim is pending, supported by its own test, or explicitly decided by a maintainer. A maintainer outcome never changes a judgment row from `judgment` / `UNVERIFIED`.

**Evidence rule:** `ENFORCED` requires a tracked negative assertion for the exact claim, a named selector, a focused command with at least one executed test, zero failures, exit zero, and a recorded violating-case reference. A passing broad suite, source-symbol search, or historical metadata does not meet this rule. Rows without accepted claim-specific evidence remain `UNVERIFIED`; `pending` explicitly means no resolution has been recorded.
