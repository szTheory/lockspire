## Redaction rules

1. **First control (DOM attribute scan):** before saving each image, the capture reads the visible text, the hidden text (a text-node walk that skips `<script>` and `<style>`), every `input`/`textarea`/`select` value, every `data-*` attribute value, and every `title`, `aria-label`, `alt` and `href` value. It runs them through the harness `scanSensitive` denylist, which is kept source-identical to `BrowserEvidence.sensitive_patterns/0` (code review IN-03). The denylist covers cookies, auth codes, JWT-looking values, token parameters, plaintext credentials, private key headers, authorization headers, verifier material, device/user codes, copy-once secrets, and production hostnames. A hit aborts the save. Result: 0 hits across 102 page loads (30 desktop, 72 changed-page).
2. **Second control (eye review):** every saved full-page image (30 desktop, 72 changed-page) was opened and reviewed by eye for keys, secrets, codes, tokens, cookies, emails beyond synthetic demo data, and production hostnames. The 14 desktop section slices are viewport crops of those reviewed pages. "Pass" means nothing sensitive is visible. Visible identifiers (`token_...`, `iat_...`, `kid_...`, `interaction_...`, `device_authorization_...`, `account_...`) are record references, not credential values. Hosts are `lockspire-ui-review.localhost` and RFC 2606 `example.com` demo hosts. No crop or recapture was needed.

## F-03: found by Plan 07, fixed in d3b2fec2, re-verified

This ID is local to this manifest. `154-RESPONSIVE-SWEEP.md` already uses F-03 for the allowed small-target note and lists this finding as the Plan 07 final-capture finding.

- **Found:** during the second-control eye review of the first final capture pass (source `768b3a61`), in `reflow320/clients.png`. On `/admin/clients` at 320 px (light and dark), the "Registered through API" provenance badge (`span.lockspire-admin-badge-provenance`, `white-space: nowrap`) ran from x=144 to x=318, past its status cluster (edge at 280) and its card (edge at 304), and stopped 2 px from the viewport edge. The cause was the two-column grid `6rem minmax(0, 1fr)` on `.lockspire-admin-client-inventory__results .lockspire-admin-status-cluster`.
- **Why the Plan 06 harness missed it:** the badge overflowed its card but not the document. `scrollWidth` equalled `clientWidth` (320) and no ancestor had `overflow: hidden`, so neither the document-overflow check nor the clipped-content check fired.
- **Fix:** commit `d3b2fec2` (`fix(154-06): wrap the app inventory status badges at phone widths`). At 640 px and below the cluster becomes `display: flex; flex-wrap: wrap`, and `CssAssertions.assert_responsive_layout!/0` guards it. Plan 07 edited no source.
- **Re-verified on `3e31d401`:** a read-only scan of all 30 paths for any element extending past its card finds 0 instances at 320, 390, 640, 768, 1119, 1120 and 1280 px (light) and at 320 px (dark) (`tmp/admin-ui-polish/v1.42/final/card-escape-scan.log`). In the new `reflow320/clients.png`, the badges wrap inside the card.
