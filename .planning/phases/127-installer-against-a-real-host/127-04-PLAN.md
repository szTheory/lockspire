<objective>
Close the installer halves of ADOPT-D05, ADOPT-D06, and ADOPT-D07 — three defects where the
library's own printed guidance is silent or wrong, and the adopter pays for it far downstream.

Purpose: `instructions/1` never mentions that a stock generated host boots `:lockspire` before the
host's own Repo, never names the supervision children Lockspire needs, and never names the
three-stage key lifecycle — the walk proved that a published key still cannot sign until it is
activated, and that JWKS being non-empty is not evidence otherwise. Separately, three in-scope
sites prescribe a bare migrate command that exits 0 against a real host while running zero of
Lockspire's 37 migrations.

Output: an `instructions/1` that names the host wiring, the key lifecycle, and the correct migrate
invocation; the two verifier remediation strings corrected to match; a new verifier check for the
supervision children; and a test pinning all of it. The installer still writes nothing into the
host's `mix.exs` or `application.ex` — zero injection is the fence, and printed instructions are
the entire library-side surface.
</objective>
